THE UPGRADE

Deep Python Pipeline & Tournament Benchmark

How We Took Orange To Production Grade

While Orange provided a great GUI baseline, standard off-the-shelf models struggle with the 33.5% Enrolled recall bottleneck. In our custom Python codebase (D:\DS AI project\src\), we engineered 17 domain interaction features, tuned XGBoost and HistGradientBoosting, and ensembled them into a production pipeline.

17 Engineered Domain Signals

Added through StudentFeatureEngineer:

  • sem1_approval_rate: approved_1 / enrolled_1
  • sem2_approval_rate: approved_2 / enrolled_2
  • grade_delta: sem2_grade - sem1_grade (Momentum)
  • financial_stress_score: 2*Debtor + 3*(1-Tuition) - 1.5*Scholarship
  • macro_misery: Unemp + Inflation - GDP

Production Tournament Arena (5-Fold CV + Held-Out Test)

Why HistGradientBoosting won: In multi-class medical and retention modeling, Macro F1 matters more than raw accuracy. HistGradientBoosting improved the minority 'Enrolled' F1 score from 0.408 (Orange) to 0.534, successfully resolving the class imbalance bottleneck!

ETHICS & ROADMAP

Benefits, Ethical AI & The 'Late Warning' Dilemma

Institutional Benefits

  • • Early identification of at-risk students before irreversible withdrawal.
  • • Targeted financial aid allocation for students blocked purely by tuition arrears.
  • • Data-backed advisor mentoring replacing subjective guesswork.

The 'Late Warning' Dilemma

Because 2nd-semester approved credits hold the highest Information Gain (0.379), predictions generated at the end of Year 1 arrive late. The next iteration must train a Semester 1 Early-Exit Model to intervene by month 3 of freshman year.

Future Research Scope

  • • Direct API integration into SRM University ERP / LMS.
  • • SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) for personalized student feature attribution.
  • • Longitudinal temporal modeling across Years 2, 3, and 4.
